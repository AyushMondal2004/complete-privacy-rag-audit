Privacy Policy Audit

Retrieves relevant sections of a mobile app privacy policy and identifies data-collection practices, with evidence and — where available — comparison against annotated ground truth.

Drop a privacy-policy .html file here, or click to choose one.
Backend: FastAPI + ChromaDB + local LLM (Ollama). Findings are generated by a language model reading retrieved policy excerpts and may be incomplete or incorrect — verify against the source policy before relying on any result.
